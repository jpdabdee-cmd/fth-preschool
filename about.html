About Us

Enrichment Center & Early Learning

A Message from Our Founder

We are a small preschool serving children ages two through six years old, excelling in science and technology. Our program is founded on the belief that the younger children benefit from modeling their older peers, while the older children benefit from being a mentor.

These relationships foster self-esteem and personal growth essential to the readiness for elementary school. Under teacher guidance, the children are given activities to continually challenge them and stimulate their individual learning process.

We have been in business for over twenty years and I can proudly say that our children are frequently recruited to attend outstanding private elementary schools. In addition, we are highly academically successful and are exceptional citizens of the community.

I want to personally invite you to call and arrange a tour to see for yourself how magical this place really is.

Sincerely,
Betty Luckett
Founder & CEO

Our Educational Approach

How we nurture young minds and hearts

1

Play-Based Learning

We believe children learn best through play. Our curriculum integrates structured and free play to develop critical thinking, creativity, and social skills.

2

Individualized Attention

Every child is unique. We maintain low student-teacher ratios to ensure each child receives personalized support and encouragement.

3

Holistic Development

We nurture the whole child—cognitive, social, emotional, and physical growth are all equally important in our programs.

4

Family Partnership

We view parents as partners in education. Open communication and family involvement are central to our philosophy.

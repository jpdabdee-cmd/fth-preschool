Frequently Asked Questions

What is your enrollment capacity?

We maintain small class sizes to ensure quality care. Our maximum capacity is 60 students across all programs, with specific ratios for each age group.

Do you offer part-time enrollment?

We offer both full-day and half-day options. Part-time enrollment (2-3 days per week) may be available depending on current capacity.

What meals do you provide?

Full-day programs include breakfast, lunch, and afternoon snack. All meals are nutritious, freshly prepared, and accommodate dietary restrictions.

Is there a waiting list?

We do maintain a waiting list for certain age groups. We recommend applying early and scheduling a tour to secure your spot.

What is your sick child policy?

Children must be fever-free for 24 hours before returning to school. We follow CDC guidelines for communicable diseases.

Do you accept subsidies or financial assistance?

We accept state subsidy programs and offer payment plans. Contact us to discuss financial assistance options.

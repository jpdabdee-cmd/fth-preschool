Our Programs

Age-appropriate learning experiences designed to nurture every child's potential

Ages 2-3

Toddler Program

Our Toddler Program provides a gentle, nurturing introduction to learning through carefully designed sensory play, music, and social interaction. We create a warm, secure environment where your little one can explore, discover, and grow at their own pace.

What Your Child Will Experience:

  • Small group sizes (maximum 8 children) for personalized attention
  • Sensory exploration activities that stimulate cognitive development
  • Early socialization in a supportive, loving environment
  • Music and movement activities to develop motor skills
  • Daily outdoor play (weather permitting)
  • Introduction to colors, shapes, and basic concepts

Program Schedule

Full Day: Monday-Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Half Day: Monday-Friday, 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Ages 3-4

Preschool Program

Our Preschool Program builds strong foundations through play-based learning, creativity, and structured activities. Children develop essential pre-literacy and numeracy skills while exploring their natural curiosity and preparing for the transition to Pre-K.

Curriculum Highlights:

  • Pre-literacy skills through storytelling and letter recognition
  • Creative expression through art, music, and dramatic play
  • Problem-solving and critical thinking activities
  • Early math concepts through hands-on exploration
  • Social-emotional development and cooperation skills
  • Outdoor learning and nature exploration

Program Schedule

Full Day: Monday-Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Half Day: Monday-Friday, 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Ages 4-5

Pre-K Program

Our comprehensive Pre-K Program prepares children for kindergarten success through advanced learning experiences, STEM activities, and independence building. We focus on academic readiness while nurturing social skills, leadership qualities, and a lifelong love of learning.

Kindergarten Readiness:

  • Advanced literacy skills: reading readiness, phonics, and writing
  • Mathematical thinking and early computation
  • STEM activities including basic science experiments
  • Leadership and teamwork development
  • Self-help skills and personal responsibility
  • Technology integration with age-appropriate tools
